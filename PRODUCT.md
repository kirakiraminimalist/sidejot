# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Dexie codebase forked from Sidejot.

## Users

The primary user has ADHD and needs to capture thoughts before attention moves elsewhere.

## Product Purpose

Capture thoughts with minimal friction, then make them retrievable by priority and due date.

## Positioning

A calm thought inbox that separates capture from organizing, so recording an idea never requires completing a complex form.

## Operating Context

Used personally in a web browser, often during moments of distraction or context switching.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Quick thought capture, optional priority, optional due date, automatic date grouping, completion, editing, deletion, filtering, and device-local persistence. Chinese is the primary interface language.

## Brand Commitments

Low-stimulation, minimal, and light green. The interface should feel supportive rather than judgmental.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials or performance claims are available and none should be invented.

## Product Principles

- Capture first; organizing can wait.
- Show the next useful decision, not every possible setting.
- Dates and urgency should be clear without creating alarm.
- Preserve privacy with local-first storage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Reduce cognitive load for ADHD users, maintain strong contrast, clear focus states, generous touch targets, and reduced-motion support.
